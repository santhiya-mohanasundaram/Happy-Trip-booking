Spotlight of the Season: Kashmir Valley

Celebrated as Paradise on Earth, winter and spring transform Kashmir into a living postcard of glistening peaks, cedar forests, and cozy houseboat firesides.

Best Winter Destination

Why Visit Kashmir Now?

Gulmarg’s powdery snow welcomes winter sports enthusiasts from across the globe, while the tranquil waters of Dal Lake offer peaceful Shikara rides against snow-capped peaks. Sip piping hot Kashmiri Kahwa infused with saffron, almonds, and cardamom while staying in a heritage cedar-wood houseboat.

Dal Houseboats Deluxe cedar heritage stays
